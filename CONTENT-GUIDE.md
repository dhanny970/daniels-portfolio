# Personalize this portfolio

The page is built from editable content in `client/src/content/portfolio.ts`. Replace `[Your name]`, the biography prompt, project titles and summaries, technology labels, process notes, credibility prompts, email address, and social URLs with details that are true for you.

Project screenshots can replace the CSS preview placeholders in `client/src/components/ProjectCard.tsx`. The cards display a live-project or source-code link only after a URL is entered in that project's `liveUrl` or `sourceUrl` field. Social links appear as placeholders until a non-empty URL is added in `socials`.

Before sharing the site, replace `your.email@example.com` with your email address and update the document title and description in `client/index.html`. Remove the optional credibility section if you do not have genuine results, credentials, or testimonials to include.
